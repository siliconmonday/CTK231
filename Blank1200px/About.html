Do You Know Silicon Monday?

Silicon Monday is a singer, songwriter, composer, and producer hailing from Orland Park, Illinois. His unique style blends contemporary alt-pop instrumentation, the razor-sharp lyricism of emo’s glory days, and the theatricality and edge of yesterday’s hard rock legends like Alice Cooper. His award-winning composition work earned him statewide recognition in the early 2020s before his debut LP, METAM, released in 2025 and standout track “Muscle Relaxer” garnered radio airplay.

Monday’s approach to composition work bridges electronic production with classical techniques. He is currently scoring IronStone Collective’s 3D puzzle platformer Stop the F, and his next project will be as composer and lead sound designer on the short film Tommy John of Tomorrow. He currently resides in Normal, Illinois where he studies audio & music production at Illinois State University.
